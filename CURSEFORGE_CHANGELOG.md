### 🐢 Floppy Turtles v1.0.0 — Initial Release (Minecraft 1.21.11 / Fabric)

Initial release of **Floppy Turtles**, bringing code-free autonomous robotic turtles to Minecraft 1.21.11!

#### ✨ What's Included:
* **Autonomous Turtle**: Full 6-axis spatial movement robot; retains items, fuel, and task state across world reloads; drops cleanly when mined.
* **Universal Fuel System**: Supports all furnace fuels (Coal, Charcoal, Blaze Rods, etc.) with a 10,000 unit capacity and live LCD fuel gauge.
* **Tool & Equipment Wear**: Simulates authentic block drops and properly degrades tool durability; scales sword damage with weapon tiers.
* **Interactive LCD Terminal**: Real-time coordinates, fuel %, status badges, range distance controls (2m–128m), and mode toggles.
* **7 Color-Coded Floppy Disks**:
  * ⛏️ **3×3 Tunnel Miner** (Royal Blue) — 3×3 tunnel boring with torch placement and anti-gravel clearing.
  * 🕳️ **Quarry 3×3** (Purple) — Layer-by-layer shaft excavation straight to bedrock.
  * 🌉 **Bridge Builder** (Orange) — 1W, 3W, or 5W (with auto guard rails) bridging across chasms and oceans.
  * 🌲 **Tree Feller** (Forest Green) — 3D BFS tree & branch felling with canopy collapse, drop vacuuming, and hill climbing.
  * 🌾 **Crop Harvester** (Golden Yellow) — 9-wide swath crop harvesting and auto-replanting with terrace climb support.
  * 🚜 **Farm Creator** (Aqua Cyan) — Builds 1 to 3 fully hydrated 9×9 tilled & seeded plots with central water reservoirs.
  * ⚔️ **Mob Sentry** (Crimson Red) — 360° perimeter defense against all hostile mobs (3m–10m range, creeper-safe, auto-loot suction).

#### ⚙️ Requirements:
* Minecraft `1.21.11`
* Fabric Loader `0.19.3+`
* Fabric API
* Java `21+`
